Technical GTM Command Center

Where should technical experts spend their time?

Solutions Architects and Forward Deployed Engineers are scarce, and today their time often goes to whoever asks loudest rather than where it moves revenue most. I built a command center that scores every account on Propensity score and Pool of funds, raises alerts on risk and opportunity, decides the right type of help for each account, and packages repeatable programs and services. It turns technical GTM capacity into a ranked, measurable investment that leadership can see and defend.
