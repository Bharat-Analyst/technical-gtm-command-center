Where should technical experts spend their time?

Solutions Architects and Forward Deployed Engineers are scarce, and today their time often goes to whoever asks loudest rather than where it moves revenue most. I built a command center that scores every account on propensity and headroom, flags risk and opportunity, routes each account to the right engagement motion, and packages repeatable programs and services. It turns technical GTM capacity into a ranked, measurable investment that leadership can see and defend.
